Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what personal data StingStock collects, why, who we share it with, and the rights you have.

1. Who We Are

StingStock publishes the Sting Stock Daily Buzz, a short daily summary of market news. The data controller for personal data processed through this website and the newsletter is Lukasz Sudol, the operator of StingStock. Contact: contact@lsudo.com.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect what we need:

  • Newsletter data: your email address (and your name, if you choose to give it) when you subscribe to the Daily Buzz.
  • Email engagement data: our email provider may record whether a message was delivered, opened, or a link was clicked, so we can keep delivery working and remove bounced addresses.
  • Technical data: when you visit the website, your browser automatically sends standard request data (such as IP address, browser type, and time of visit) to the services that deliver the page (see section 4).
  • Messages you send us: the content of any email you send to us and your address.

3. How and Why We Use It

  • Sending the newsletter — based on your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). You can withdraw consent at any time using the unsubscribe link in every email or by contacting us.
  • Keeping emails deliverable and the website running securely — based on our legitimate interest in operating a reliable service (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Answering your messages — based on our legitimate interest in responding to you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

We do not sell your personal data, use it for advertising, or make automated decisions about you that have legal or similarly significant effects.

4. Service Providers

We share data only with providers that help us run StingStock:

  • SendGrid (Twilio Inc.) — sends the newsletter and other emails. It receives your email address and the engagement data described above and may use it only to provide its service to us.
  • GitHub Pages (GitHub Inc.) — hosts this website and processes technical request data, such as IP addresses, to deliver pages and protect the service.
  • Content delivery networks (cdn.tailwindcss.com, unpkg.com) — deliver the styling and 3D graphics libraries this website loads, and therefore receive your IP address and browser data when you visit.

We may also disclose data if required by law or to protect our legal rights.

5. International Transfers

Some of these providers are based in the United States. Where data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, we rely on appropriate safeguards, such as the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses.

6. How Long We Keep It

We keep your newsletter data until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. After you unsubscribe, we may keep your email address on a suppression list only to make sure you are not emailed again. Emails you send us are kept for as long as needed to handle your request. Hosting and CDN providers keep technical logs according to their own retention policies.

7. Cookies and Tracking

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics or advertising trackers. Emails may contain standard open and click tracking provided by our email provider, as described above.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you and get a copy of it;
  • have inaccurate data corrected;
  • have your data deleted;
  • restrict or object to our processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, without affecting processing done before withdrawal.

To use any of these rights, email contact@lsudo.com. We will reply within one month. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in particular in the country where you live or work.

9. Security

The website is served over HTTPS, and we limit access to personal data to what is needed to run the service. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your data.

10. Children

StingStock is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has given us their data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make significant changes, we will let newsletter subscribers know by email.
